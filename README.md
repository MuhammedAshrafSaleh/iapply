## 📖 About

**iApply** is a professional frontend website for an HR Consulting and Recruitment agency. Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it presents the company's services, philosophy, values, and customer portfolio in a clean, modern single-page layout.

> *"Land Your Dream Job Right Now"*

---

## ✨ Features

- 🦸 **Hero Section** — Bold headline with a strong call-to-action button
- 🛠️ **Services** — HR Consultancy, Legal Services & Recruitment/Outsourcing
- 👥 **About Us** — Company background and history
- 🧭 **Philosophy** — Vision & Mission statements
- 💡 **Our Values** — Integrity, Quality & Excellence, Customer Care, Technology
- 🤝 **Our Customers** — Client logos showcase
- 📞 **Contact Form** — Full contact form with Name, Title, Company, Email, Phone & Message fields
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Works on all screen sizes
